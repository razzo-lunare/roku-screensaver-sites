Animated Stars support

Help with Animated Stars

Email thomas.cocozzello@gmail.com with Animated Stars in the subject. Include the app version, Roku model, Roku OS version and a description of the issue. Find model and software information under Settings → System → About. Omit passwords, payment details, account numbers, device identifiers and household network information.

Customize playback

Open Settings → Theme → Screensavers → Animated Stars → Change screensaver settings. Choose Violet Mountains, Twilight Lake, Desert Mesa or Open Sky. Change horizon color and star speed, toggle constellation highlights and names independently, or adjust decorative effects and the local clock. Choose Done, then Preview to see saved changes. Menu wording can vary by Roku OS.

Reduced Motion and leaving playback

Reduced Motion slows the sky and pauses aurora, shooting stars and twinkle. Press a remote button to dismiss the screensaver; playback controls and preferences are in the separate settings screen.

Why does the sky differ from outside?

Hipparcos star patterns appear in an imagined rotating sky. Animated Stars does not request your location and is not an observing guide. Plain sky between constellation reveals is expected. Aurora and shooting stars are decorative effects.

Playback works offline; connecting to the internet does not turn it into a live sky view. Store availability is not confirmed. See the privacy notice, terms of use and credits and licenses.
