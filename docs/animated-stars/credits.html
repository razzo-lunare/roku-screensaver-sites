Star catalog and app notices

Star positions and brightness
ESA Hipparcos mission. Hipparcos New Reduction by Floor van Leeuwen (2007), CDS I/311. Public domain.

Artwork
Landscapes and constellation figures created for Animated Stars. The terrain foregrounds and menu cover were generated with OpenAI ImageGen from written briefs.
The shooting-star trail reuses original Animated Aurora artwork.
The constellation stroke is original artwork drawn by the Animated Stars background generator.
The aurora curtains reuse original Animated Aurora artwork and motion.

About this sky
2,486 catalog stars and 30 constellation figures in an imagined rotating sky.
This is not a current view from your location or an observing guide. Catalog
directions use epoch J1991.25; the app does not apply current proper motion.
Constellation connectors are original illustrations, not IAU boundaries.
Aurora and shooting stars are decorative effects, not forecasts or observations.

Privacy
Playback makes no network requests and has no accounts, ads, analytics, or
location access. Preferences stay in the Roku registry. The clock uses the
device's local time. Roku's platform services are governed by Roku's policies.

Artwork notices

The atmospheric gradients, glow sprite, and constellation stroke were drawn by the
tools/stars/render-backgrounds.mjs generator. The Violet Mountains, Twilight
Lake, and Desert Mesa foregrounds were generated with OpenAI ImageGen from
written briefs.
The Animated Stars cover was generated with OpenAI ImageGen from a written
brief. The two packaged sizes derive from the same cover. No stock art,
reference photographs, or third-party font files are included. Playback
and settings use the Roku system font.

The meteor trail is original repository artwork from Animated Aurora,
generated by tools/aurora/generate-effects.mjs.
The eight aurora curtain textures are original Animated Aurora artwork
generated by tools/aurora/generate-effects.mjs.
