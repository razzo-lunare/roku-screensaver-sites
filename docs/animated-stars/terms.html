What the sky represents

Animated Stars provides decorative, simulated sky imagery. Star directions use Hipparcos catalog epoch J1991.25 without current proper-motion adjustment. Constellation connectors are original illustrations, not IAU boundaries. The rotating sky is not a current view from your location or an observing guide. Aurora and shooting stars are decorative, not observations or forecasts.

Do not rely on the scene for observing plans, navigation, weather, safety decisions or precise scientific measurements. No forecast, current-sky accuracy, minimum frame rate or uninterrupted operation is promised.
