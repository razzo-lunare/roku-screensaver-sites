Artwork notices

validation-planet.png is original procedural artwork created for this repository.
It is a synthetic shaded disk, not a planetary texture or astronomical dataset.

To the extent possible under law, the contributors waive all copyright and
related or neighboring rights to validation-planet.png under CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Generator: tools/generate-validation-asset.mjs (Node.js standard library only).
The validation asset uses no external imagery or fonts. Other assets are
described below. Roku supplies the system fonts.

Planet disks in images/planets/ (except the AI-generated sun.png, procedural moon.png and USGS-derived mercury.png) are derived
by tools/animated-solar-system/render-assets.py from the NASA/JPL-Caltech Solar System
Simulator map collection. Credit NASA/JPL-Caltech and USGS where indicated.
Exact source URLs and input SHA-256 hashes are in data/asset-provenance.json.
JPL image-use policy: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy/
Catalogue and individual map ownership: https://maps.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/
No NASA/JPL logos or endorsement is implied. These are processed representative
maps, not scientific-analysis products or current cloud observations. Venus uses
a visible-cloud Mariner-based map, not Magellan radar surface imagery. Saturn
rings and Sun glow are original procedural illustrations. Fixed lighting,
rotation, and ring opening are illustrative rather than epoch-correct.

moon.png is an original procedural shaded/cratered gray illustration generated
by tools/animated-solar-system/render-assets.py, released under CC0 1.0 Universal. It is not
a lunar surface map or a calculation of current phase/orientation.

settings-panel.png, settings-focus.png, settings-footprint.png and
sun-glow.png are original procedural assets from tools/animated-solar-system/render-interface.mjs,
released under CC0 1.0 Universal. The Sun is a representative textured illustration
with decorative glow, not an observation or simulation of current solar activity.

cover-fhd.jpg and cover-hd.jpg: AI-generated Animated Solar System selection-cover artwork,
created with OpenAI image generation on 2026-10-17 for this project, then resized
for Roku menu icons. Illustrative composition; not validated astronomy or a
planetary texture. No third-party stock image, NASA logo or endorsement used.
Used as selection artwork only, never loaded into passive playback.

sun.png: AI-generated representative solar photosphere illustration created
with OpenAI image generation on 2026-10-17 for Animated Solar System. Technical export by
tools/animated-solar-system/export-sun.py. No third-party stock image or NASA logo was used.
Surface granulation, spots and limb darkening are illustrative, not observed or
current solar activity. Source/export hashes are in data/sun-artwork.json.

images/lighting/: unshaded fixed-view planet surfaces are derived from the same
NASA/JPL-Caltech/USGS source maps and reviewed source hashes listed above, not
new observations. Neutral Moon craters, split schematic Saturn rings and shared
Lambert shadow masks are original code-generated assets released under CC0 1.0.
See data/lighting-provenance.json for individual asset/source hashes and limits.
No NASA endorsement, current atmosphere, eclipse, axial rotation or accurate
ring-shadow claim is made.

Mercury uses the USGS Astrogeology Science Center's May 2013
"Mercury MESSENGER MDIS Global Mosaic 250m", distributed as its official
1024x513 JPEG image. The original product's 250 m/pixel resolution does not
apply to the downsampled image or runtime textures.
Credit: USGS Astrogeology Science Center; Arizona State University and the
MESSENGER Team. Polar mosaics: C. Ernst and N. Chabot; global mosaic:
Applied Coherent Technology (ACT) Corporation.
Catalogue and reuse terms:
https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mercury_messenger_mdis_global_mosaic_250m
The catalogue states Access Constraints: Public domain;
Use Constraints: Please cite authors (verified October 19, 2026).
The historical global monochrome mosaic includes north/south polar averages
and high-incidence infill. It contains residual imaging/illumination seams;
it is not a current observation or a photometrically pure albedo map.
Equirectangular, north-up, east-positive, centered at 0 degrees longitude,
verified against the catalogue and accompanying PDS3 map label. No crop,
mirroring or longitude roll is applied. Exact source URL/hash, dimensions,
rights evidence and map label are recorded in data/asset-provenance.json.
Derived sphere images and the small rotation map retain this attribution.
No NASA, USGS, ASU, MESSENGER or other endorsement is implied.

orbit-stroke.png is an original procedural alpha-coverage strip generated by
tools/animated-solar-system/render-interface.mjs, released under CC0 1.0 Universal.
Sphere projection, antialiased limbs and schematic Saturn ring layers are
prepared by host tools/animated-solar-system/appearance.py; map ownership remains as above.

Uranus/Neptune ring sprites: original procedural analytic bands by this project.
No NASA/Webb image pixels were copied. Scientific design references:
https://science.nasa.gov/uranus/
https://science.nasa.gov/missions/webb/new-webb-image-captures-clearest-view-of-neptunes-rings-in-decades/
Planes, band widths and visibility are illustrative, not current observation.

Ring material values: NASA NSSDCA planetary ring fact sheets (Saturn, Uranus,
Neptune, Jupiter). Pole coefficients: NASA/JPL NAIF pck00011.tpc. Scientific
constants are reproduced with source attribution in data/lighting-provenance.json;
no SPICE runtime or third-party code is bundled. Scattering coefficients and
exposure transfer are project approximations, not NASA measurements.

Localized menu illustrations in images/branding/*.jpg are AI-generated edits
of the project's existing cover, created with the built-in image-generation
tool. They are menu branding only, not astronomy textures. No external font or
new third-party imagery was bundled. Titles: Sistema Solar Animado, Système solaire animé, Animiertes Sonnensystem.

Earth rotation map (lighting/earth-map.jpg): filtered derivative of the same
reviewed NASA/JPL/USGS historic Earth mosaic used by planets/earth.png.
Source, source checksum, credit and limitations: data/earth-rotation.json.
No new imagery license or current orientation/weather claim.

Source-specific map acknowledgments (including derived lighting/Earth maps):
Courtesy NASA/JPL-Caltech. Earth and Mars: Caltech/JPL/USGS.
Venus, Jupiter and Uranus maps: creator David Seal; owner JPL/Caltech.
Saturn and Neptune maps: creator Don Davis; owner JPL/Caltech.
Saturn, Uranus and Neptune source maps include fictional illustrative material.
Individual catalogue URLs and source image hashes: data/asset-provenance.json.
NASA, JPL, Caltech, USGS, Roku and OpenAI do not sponsor or endorse Animated Solar System.

Current-orientation globe map derivatives (0.4.24): images/lighting/*-map.jpg
use the exact reviewed source maps listed in data/asset-provenance.json.
Generator: tools/animated-solar-system/export-planet-rotation.py (and export-earth-map.py).
Current IAU body-frame orientation is computed separately from the historical
map pixels. Venus clouds and giant-planet features are illustrative, not current
atmospheres or tracked storm longitudes; JPL map longitude registration outside
Mercury/Mars/Earth is a representative assumption. Rings remain schematic.
Rotation constants: NASA/JPL NAIF PCK00011, IAU WGCCRE 2015.
https://naif.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/naif/generic_kernels/pck/pck00011.tpc
Bundled factual coefficients and source hash: data/planet-rotation.json.

Runtime rendering buffer templates in data/rendering/ are original functional
blank-pixel data generated by tools/animated-solar-system/render-templates.mjs. They contain
no external artwork or fonts. Runtime surface pixels continue to use the same
reviewed NASA/JPL-Caltech and USGS maps credited above. Repacking those pixels
as temporary BMPV4 images changes their encoding, not their source or rights.


Animated Solar System branding update (2026-10-24): cover-fhd.jpg,
cover-hd.jpg and images/branding/solar-system-{es,fr,de,it}-{fhd,hd}.jpg
are built-in OpenAI ImageGen text edits of this project's existing selection
cover. Five master PNGs and the exact edit prompts are retained in
design/animated-solar-system/artwork/ and BRANDING.md outside the runtime.
Spanish and Portuguese share the identical title artwork. Italian has its own
"Sistema Solare Animato" cover. No new third-party artwork or font was added.
The illustrations are promotional covers, not astronomy data or screenshots.
