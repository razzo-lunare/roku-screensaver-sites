Animated Solar System policies

Privacy notice

Proposed policy · No effective date set · Publisher: Tom Cocozzello

This notice explains information handling for the Animated Solar System Roku screensaver, this website and support requests sent to the publisher.

The screensaver

The screensaver uses astronomy data and images included with the app. Its code does not send personal information to the publisher and contains no analytics, advertising, account-login or payment service. It does not request your name, email, location or a personal account.

Your Roku's local clock and language are used on the device. Display choices, such as brightness, names, motion and clock format, are saved locally in the device registry. They are not sent to the publisher by the app.

Technical diagnostics may be available through Roku's developer console. The screensaver does not upload them. If you choose to send diagnostics to support, remove passwords, signing keys, account details and other private information first.

This website

The pages themselves do not set cookies or use analytics, advertising pixels, embedded third-party media, external fonts or contact forms.

Hosting provider: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Hosting and network services can process connection information, such as an IP address and requested page, when serving a website.

GitHub Pages logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including signed-out visitors. GitHub controls its hosting/security processing and retention under its privacy statement; we do not promise a fixed deletion period for GitHub's logs.

Provider privacy information: GitHub privacy statement.

When you contact support

If you email support, the publisher receives your email address, the contents of your message and any attachments you choose to provide. This information is used to answer the request, troubleshoot problems and manage related security or legal issues. Do not send account passwords, payment information or signing keys.

Review item: the publisher has not yet confirmed the email service provider and its privacy information.

Access to support messages: Tom Cocozzello is the only person with access to the support inbox. The email service provider processes messages as described in its privacy information.

Retention and deletion: We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to address requests and meet applicable legal obligations. We periodically review retained messages and delete or anonymize information no longer needed.

Roku and other services

Roku operates the device, account and Streaming Store under its own policies. This notice describes the publisher's app, website and support practices; it does not describe all information processed by your Roku device or account.

Links to source institutions and service-provider policies open other websites. Those services apply their own privacy practices.

Privacy requests and security

Contact thomas.cocozzello@gmail.com to ask about information you have sent to the publisher, request correction or deletion, or report a privacy concern. Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights. The publisher may need enough information to verify a request and may retain information where required by law.

Please provide only information needed to handle your request. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

The screensaver has no account or form for submitting personal information. If you believe a child sent personal information to support, contact thomas.cocozzello@gmail.com so the publisher can review the request and applicable obligations.

Changes to this notice

Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. The published notice must reflect the app and services actually in use.
