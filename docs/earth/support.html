EARTH support

Help with EARTH

Email thomas.cocozzello@gmail.com with EARTH in the subject. Include your Roku model, Roku OS version, EARTH version and a description of the issue. Never send passwords, signing keys, private account details or household network credentials.

Where is the ISS?

The station can be hidden behind Earth. It also hides when a usable local prediction is unavailable or outside the seven-day window around the bundled orbital-element date. That date is recorded in external settings under Credits & Licenses, Copyright & about EARTH. A newer build with newer elements is required to extend coverage; connecting the Roku to the internet does not refresh the elements inside EARTH.

Reduced motion freezes the station. The orbit-line setting hides only the guide. Real time uses device UTC; 2x, 5x and 10x preview advance predicted ISS time. The Moon, Earth sunlight and local clock continue to use actual device time.

Lighting and imagery

The day/night boundary and Moon phase follow the device date. Historical globe imagery is not a weather camera. The view has a fixed sample orientation, and the Moon is an illustrative inset. Lighting prepares after startup and refreshes each minute; if it fails, a dim globe or the previous usable frame remains.

Settings and dismissal

On the TV, open Settings → Theme → Screensavers → EARTH (or the development entry), then Change screensaver settings. Use the directional pad and OK for the separate settings screen. During playback, a remote button dismisses the screensaver.
