EARTH policies

Privacy notice

Proposed policy · No effective date set · Publisher: Tom Cocozzello

This notice describes the EARTH Roku screensaver, its pages on this website and support requests. Animated Solar System has its own separate privacy notice.

On your Roku

EARTH playback makes no network requests. Images and dated ISS orbital elements are bundled with the app; sunlight, Moon phase and ISS predictions are calculated locally. The developer may download fresh orbital elements before building an update. Your Roku does not contact that data provider through EARTH.

EARTH has no account sign-in, advertising, analytics or payment service. It does not collect your location or send identifiers, preferences, playback timestamps or other personal information to the publisher. It uses the device clock locally. Star twinkle, reduced motion, guide visibility and ISS preview speed are saved in the Roku's local registry.

Development diagnostics can appear in Roku's local developer console. EARTH does not upload them. If you send diagnostics to support, remove passwords, signing keys, account details and other private information first.

This website

These static pages contain no analytics, advertising pixels, contact forms, external fonts or embedded third-party media, and do not set cookies. Images and styles are served by this website.

Hosting provider: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub Pages logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including signed-out visitors. GitHub controls its hosting/security processing and retention under its privacy statement; we do not promise a fixed deletion period for GitHub's logs. See the GitHub privacy statement. The no-network statement about playback does not mean visiting this website creates no hosting requests or logs.

When you email support

If you choose to email thomas.cocozzello@gmail.com, the publisher receives your email address, message and attachments. They are used to respond, troubleshoot and address relevant security or legal issues. Do not include account passwords, payment information or signing keys.

Email service provider: Google (Gmail). Support messages and attachments are received and stored using Gmail. Google processes email content and delivery information to provide the service and help detect spam and abuse. Google Privacy Policy · Gmail privacy information · Google data retention information.

Google controls its own processing and retention, including backup copies, under those policies. Deleting a message from the support inbox does not guarantee immediate removal from all Google systems. The publisher’s support-retention practice below is separate from Google’s processing.

Tom Cocozzello is the only person with access to the support inbox. The email service provider processes messages as described in its privacy information.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to address requests and meet applicable legal obligations. We periodically review retained messages and delete or anonymize information no longer needed.

For questions about information you have sent, or to request access, correction or deletion where applicable, contact thomas.cocozzello@gmail.com. Hosting and email providers control their own processing; the publisher cannot promise a deletion time for their logs or backups.

Roku and other links

Roku operates its devices, accounts, app distribution and updates under its own privacy policy. External links open services with their own policies. This notice does not describe those services as part of EARTH.

Changes

This proposed notice has no effective date. An approved version will identify its effective date and applicable practices. Contact thomas.cocozzello@gmail.com with EARTH privacy questions.
