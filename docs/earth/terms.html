What the scene represents

Earth and city-light maps are historical composites. Sunlight and Moon phase are approximations based on the device clock. The fixed camera is not your detected location, and the Moon is an illustrative inset. ISS artwork is enlarged and its attitude and small structural details are illustrative.

ISS positions are offline SGP4 predictions from dated bundled elements, not received spacecraft telemetry. The ISS and guide hide outside seven days of the element epoch; an updated build is needed for new elements. Maneuvers and changing atmospheric drag can cause errors even within that window. Faster preview settings advance only ISS time.

Do not rely on the scene for navigation, safety decisions, observing plans or precise scientific measurements. The local clock display does not establish that all imagery or orbital data is current.
