The atmospheric gradients, glow sprite, and constellation stroke were drawn by the
tools/stars/render-backgrounds.mjs generator. The Violet Mountains, Twilight
Lake, and Desert Mesa foregrounds were generated with OpenAI ImageGen from
written briefs.
The Animated Stars cover was generated with OpenAI ImageGen from a written
brief. The two packaged sizes derive from the same cover. No stock art,
reference photographs, or third-party font files are included. Playback
and settings use the Roku system font.

The meteor trail is original repository artwork from Animated Aurora,
generated by tools/aurora/generate-effects.mjs.
The eight aurora curtain textures are original Animated Aurora artwork
generated by tools/aurora/generate-effects.mjs.
