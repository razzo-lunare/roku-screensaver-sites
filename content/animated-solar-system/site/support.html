If Animated Solar System is installed on your Roku

  1. On your TV, press Home on the physical Roku remote.
  2. Open Settings → Theme → Screensavers.
  3. Select Animated Solar System and open Preview. The name may be localized as Sistema Solar Animado, Système solaire animé or Animiertes Sonnensystem.
  4. Use Change screensaver settings to adjust display options or open Credits.

These menu labels follow Roku's documentation; translations and available controls can vary. If your menus differ, report the labels you see. A standalone screensaver may not appear as a Home-screen app tile.

Common questions

How do I leave the screensaver?

Press a button on the physical Roku remote. Screensaver playback is passive; it does not use remote input to navigate the solar system.

Why are the planet sizes and distances different from reality?

The overview is schematic so the planets remain visible on a television. Historical maps represent surfaces or clouds; they are not live imagery.

Why is orbital movement subtle?

Real time follows very slow astronomical motion. The separate Planet speed setting can accelerate orbital time. Planet rotation controls surface motion independently. Changing these preferences does not accelerate the local clock.

Does it need the internet?

The screensaver bundles its app content and astronomy tables. Roku's own installation, account and store services may still require a connection.

Report a problem

Send the Roku model, Roku OS version, app version if known, affected setting, what you expected and what appeared. If possible, describe the steps that reproduce the issue. A photo is optional; keep household and account details out of it. Do not send passwords, signing keys, account tokens or full device logs.

If settings becomes unresponsive, press Home and tell support the last selected row. Do not factory-reset the device or delete preferences solely for troubleshooting this app.
