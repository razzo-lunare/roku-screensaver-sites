two historical map products, not current Earth observations.

moon.png: orthographic near-side projection of the NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit 2025
color map, assembled from LRO Wide Angle Camera photographs with polar LOLA
albedo fill. Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio; Ernie Wright (USRA),
LRO/LROC and LOLA teams, Arizona State University. Public domain under SVS
reproduction guidance; this item has no noted third-party restriction.
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/#frequently-asked-questions
NASA's mosaic is color/exposure adjusted for appearance, with some inpainted
gaps; it is not a scientific-analysis product. Runtime phase lighting follows
the device UTC date using a low-order Sun/Moon longitude approximation.
120 original procedural black-alpha masks (images/moon-phase/*.png) quantize
elongation to 3 degrees. They are CC0 1.0 artwork under the dedication below.
The photographic near-side surface stays fixed. Since 0.1.21 the phase mask
rotates to align its lit limb with the Earth-camera Sun direction; the dated
geocentric phase fraction is preserved. This is an illustrative inset, not
common 3D Earth/Moon geometry or a local sky view. No local horizon tilt,
libration or topographic shadows are simulated.
Astronomy formula reference: Paul Schlyter, Computing planetary positions,
https://stjarnhimlen.se/comp/ppcomp.html (mean elements and dominant terms).
Original project implementation; no third-party source code is bundled.
Source URL/hash, map convention and limitations are in data/asset-provenance.json.

Current Earth lighting (0.1.8): earth-day.png (1000x1000) and earth-night.png
(1000x1000 since 0.1.14) are separate orthographic derivatives of the same reviewed daytime
and Black Marble maps credited above. Version 0.1.14 replaces the low-resolution daytime map with the 8192x4096
Blue Marble 2002 map. Version 0.1.17 uses its cloud-free Land Surface, Ocean
Color, and Sea Ice product (8192x4096 PNG), replacing the cloudy TIFF.
Land observations are June–September 2001; this is not current weather. Night imagery remains Black Marble 2016.
Runtime night alpha is original procedural rendering using USNO approximate
solar coordinates and Greenwich mean sidereal time:
https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/sun_approx
https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/GAST
The implementation uses UTC as an approximation to UT1, not TDB, and omits
Earth-orientation corrections, refraction and physical twilight scattering.
The 25 N / 95 W sample camera stays fixed; no user location is inferred.
City-light imagery remains the 2016 historical composite, not live electricity
or weather data. Lighting refreshes once per UTC minute independently of ISS
preview speed. The unused baked-light prototype is host-only since 0.1.14.
