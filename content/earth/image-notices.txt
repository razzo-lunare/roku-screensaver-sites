moon.png: orthographic near-side projection of the NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit 2025
color map, assembled from LRO Wide Angle Camera photographs with polar LOLA
albedo fill. Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio; Ernie Wright (USRA),
LRO/LROC and LOLA teams, Arizona State University. Public domain under SVS
reproduction guidance; this item has no noted third-party restriction.
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/#frequently-asked-questions
NASA's mosaic is color/exposure adjusted for appearance, with some inpainted
gaps; it is not a scientific-analysis product. Our phase lighting is illustrative,
not date-correct; no simulated libration or topographic shadows are claimed.
Source URL/hash, map convention and limitations are in data/asset-provenance.json.
